Apico Blood Pressure

The simplest way to track your blood pressure every day.

  • Log systolic, diastolic and heart rate in seconds
  • Weekly insights and a smooth trend chart
  • Full history and PDF export
  • Reminders, Home Screen widget and Apple Watch companion
  • Your data stays on your device and in Apple Health

Apico is a logging tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always talk to your doctor about your readings.
